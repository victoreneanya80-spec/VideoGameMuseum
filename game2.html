About the Game

In Super Mario Bros., players guide Mario through the Mushroom Kingdom to rescue Princess Toadstool from Bowser, the King of the Koopas. The adventure spans 8 worlds with 4 levels each, filled with pipes, hidden blocks, power-ups and enemies like Goombas and Koopa Troopas.

Koji Kondo's soundtrack, especially the overworld theme, is among the most recognizable music in video game history.

Why It Belongs in the Museum

  • It set the template for the side-scrolling platform game.
  • World 1-1 is still studied as a model of teaching players through level design instead of instructions.
  • It helped revive the home console market in North America after the 1983 video game crash.
  • Secrets like warp zones rewarded curiosity and exploration.
