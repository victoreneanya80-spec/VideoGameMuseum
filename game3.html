About the Game

Ocarina of Time follows Link, a boy from Kokiri Forest, on a quest to stop the Gerudo king Ganondorf from seizing the Triforce. Partway through, Link travels seven years into the future and must move between childhood and adulthood to save the land of Hyrule.

Music is central to the adventure: songs played on the ocarina open doors, change day to night, call Link's horse Epona and warp him across the map.

Why It Belongs in the Museum

  • Its Z-targeting lock-on system became the standard for 3D melee combat.
  • It was the first 3D game in the Zelda series and showed how to build a large 3D world.
  • Context-sensitive buttons let one button do many things depending on the situation.
  • It is regularly ranked among the greatest video games ever made.
